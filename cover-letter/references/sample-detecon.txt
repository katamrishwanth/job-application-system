Dear Maja,

I am writing to express my interest in the Consultant AI & Digital Strategy position at Detecon. As a Master in Management graduate from ESCP Business School with a mechanical engineering background from IIT, I have spent the last few years working at the point where business decisions meet the data and technology needed to support them. Detecon's approach of pairing management consulting with genuine technological depth is the environment I have been looking for.

What draws me to this work is that I like building the thing, not only recommending it. At Procter & Gamble, I rebuilt the DACH discounter sellout data model, translating business requirements into technical requirements for an external DevOps team, proposing two implementation approaches, and setting naming conventions and dimensions in the data platforms so the KPIs could not be misread downstream. This corrected a 30 to 80 percent misrepresentation in business KPIs and made the numbers dependable for commercial decisions. In a parallel project, I mapped analytics tool needs across commercial functions through 1:1 interviews, retired redundant tools, and scoped an AI assistant to guide users to the right ones, saving an estimated 720 hours a year. Both taught me that the hard part of digital transformation is rarely the technology; it is getting functions to agree on what good looks like.

I have carried that interest into my own work. I recently designed and self-hosted an n8n workflow with an AI agent on Ollama models that turns a photo of a construction invoice into structured cost data and live KPIs, replacing a manual ledger, and I built the whole setup to run at zero cost. Earlier, at Libergy, I built a B2B platform from scratch on Bubble with CSRD reporting, emissions tracking, and automated invoicing, and automated tender monitoring across 16 platforms in Power Automate. My consulting foundation came from leading a six-person team at 180 Degrees Consulting, where I owned scoping, risks, and delivery cadence for a multi-campus European institution, and from a live-client project with Quantis, a BCG company.

Detecon appeals to me for two concrete reasons. First, the Deutsche Telekom and T-Systems backing means the AI and IT strategy questions here are asked at real enterprise scale rather than in pilots. Second, your energy and transport and logistics practices connect directly to my thesis on decarbonising German long-haul trucking, where I built the TCO and LCA models myself.
