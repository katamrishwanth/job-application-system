## Pricing model
- **Cost side:** decomposed the full biofuel value chain and summed cost
  contributions at each stage
- **Revenue side:** calculated how much premium could be charged over diesel
  while staying competitive with the cost of buying carbon certificates for
  equivalent carbon reduction
- **Output:** defined the viable price band for B2B commercial pricing of HVO

## Regulatory research
Researched in depth:
- **RED II** (Renewable Energy Directive): 32% renewable target by 2030, 14%
  transport sub-target, 3.5% advanced biofuels sub-target
- **EU ETS** (Emissions Trading System): carbon pricing mechanism
- **BEHG** (Brennstoffemissionshandelsgesetz): German national fuel carbon levy
- **nEHS** (national Emissions Trading System): extension of BEHG
- **EED** (Energy Efficiency Directive)
- **CSRD** (Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive)

### CSRD specifically (the strongest single Libergy asset)
- Identified CSRD as the most pressing regulatory need for Libergy's customers,
  due to recency, complexity, and lack of customer knowledge
- Evaluated the full sustainability reporting process to locate where CSRD applies
- Researched different implementation timelines (large companies vs. SMEs vs.
  listed SMEs)
- Examined biofuel-specific reporting requirements in detail
- **Delivered a CSRD compliance module inside Libergy's digital platform:**
  customer inputs required details, platform generates the compliance report
- Mechanism framing that works well: *regulation in, guided questions and a
  generated compliance report out*
