  (Winning at Grocers) customer meeting on 3 September

  market dynamics

- Same insights re-used in the Aldi customer meeting (cross-retailer application)

### Assortment analysis
- Used the ABC Assortment Booster tool, found its limitations, and re-ran the
  full analysis with closer collaboration to match actual decision-making needs
- Manually mapped every private label (PL) SKU using FastMart data, physical
  store visits, and desk research
- **Recommendation delivered:** add the Ultimate Night size to the shelf range
  (SRP) at discounters
- Presented in deliberately simple slides for commercial leadership

### Consumer survey
- Worked with a new service provider: set up intro call, learned the platform,
  executed the survey
- Used all 3 behavioural purchase constraints for precise targeting (the most
  sophisticated use of that panel's capability)
- Proactively proposed a dummy survey first to preview quality, which led to
  high-quality final results
- Secured alignment from Kevin Schumacher and Flo on panel groups and goals
- **Three insights found:**
  1. Affinity index opportunity: capturing drug store PL buyers at Lidl
  2. Age-based opportunity: capturing 55+ Tena brand loyalists (habitual
     buyers) through awareness at Lidl
  3. Low satisfaction among Lidl PL buyers, a retention risk and an opportunity
- Self-upskilled on brand building by reading *How Brands Grow* (Byron Sharp);
  applied penetration-over-loyalty thinking (double jeopardy law) to frame
  the recommendations
