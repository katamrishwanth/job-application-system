## The problem (as quantified in the investment case)

**Primary problems in India's LPG supply chain:**
1. **Accessibility:** delivery wait times of 3–15 days; rural/urban imbalance
2. **Affordability:** ~80 million BPL (Below Poverty Line) households unable to
   afford full refill amounts

**Secondary problems:**
- 110 million consumers on Domestic Bulk Connection (DBC, double cylinder)
  = **₹220B blocked in excess inventory**
- **₹386B/year lost by OMCs** (Oil Marketing Companies) maintaining excess
  unused cylinders ← this is the headline figure used on the CV
- 1% pilferage = **₹15B** government subsidy loss
- No demand forecasting at dealer or consumer level
- 470 million cylinders needed to serve 240 million consumers in transit logistics

## The solution
IoT-based supply chain platform:
- **Pay-and-Use (PAU) technology:** weight-based sensors on cylinders enabling
  per-use metering, removing the need for full upfront refill payment
- **Automated demand registration:** real-time booking when gas level drops
  below threshold
- **Data management:** cloud-based tracking of consumption, demand forecasting
  at dealer and consumer level

## The MVP
- Built using customer data (consumption patterns) and operational data
  (delivery logistics, inventory levels)
- **Reduced inventory inefficiency by 58%** by eliminating excess cylinder
  requirements through demand forecasting
- Enabled DBC elimination, the single biggest inventory cost driver

## Business model
- **B2B:** sold to OMCs (3 public OMCs) and ~20,000 LPG dealers
- Revenue streams: product sales + service charges + channel partnership with OMC
- Additional revenue: cloud computing and data analytics services

## Market sizing
- 80 million consumers addressable for the Pay-As-You-Use solution
- 200 million consumers for the supply chain automation solution
- 8–10% assumed annual penetration rate
