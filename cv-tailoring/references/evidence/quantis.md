## Research question

"Why are circular business models in the sports and fashion industry so hard to
implement and maintain?"

**Prior context:** built on a 2022 Quantis x ESCP project that produced an
Extended Sustainable Business Model Canvas. The 2023 brief was to make those
insights *actionable* by turning them into a diagnostic tool.

## Methodology

1. **Literature review** mapping 4 types of circular business model (CBM):
   - Longevity & Durability
   - Access-based (rental / sharing)
   - Collection & Resale
   - Recycling & Reuse of Materials
2. Identified enablers and barriers per CBM type, split internal vs. external
3. **Framework built on:** Sustainable Business Model Canvas + RCOV framework
4. Framework components: Value Proposition (central), Stakeholders, Activities,
   Internal & External Organisation, Impact (Environmental / Social / Financial)
5. **Outreach:** 75+ companies contacted, 5 company interviews secured
6. Plus 2 industry experts (one from OECD) = **7 expert interviews total**

## Findings

**Top enablers**
- Well-established brand trust
- Internal cross-departmental cooperation
- Aligned stakeholders
- Proof-of-concept experimentation
- Design for disassembly

**Top barriers**
- Path dependency of existing processes and mentalities
- Lower margins than linear models
- Unpredictable take-back capacity planning
- Lack of recycling knowledge and technology
- Consumer preference to own rather than rent

**Deliverable:** a descriptive diagnostic framework CBM managers can use to map
their business model and identify success/failure factors.

**Stated limitation:** validated as a useful descriptive tool, but analytical
depth requires further development. (Honest limitation, good interview material.)
